About

The yearly goals are tracked against an even pace through the year (goal × days so far ÷ days in the year). "Needed" is the weekly average the rest of the year takes; the projection adds the planned miles in plan.csv (as time, at the last 28 days' average pace), then the last 28 days' average, to what's done. Both count runs only (hand-made FIT files too), never hikes or skis. Time is moving time, distance × average pace. Time is the headline because a trail mile takes longer than a road mile, so a mileage goal quietly pulls toward roads.

Each milestone is a finish time on a flat course at sea level, judged conservatively: a race reaches it when its clock time beats the milestone time at its altitude, converted by whichever published method gives the least adjustment (Daniels' 4 or 5 s/mi per 1,000 ft above 3,000 ft, or the NCAA 2009 tables), and hills earn nothing. Predictions and VDOTs on this page are the Fitness tab's (clock time, the methods averaged). The bars fill as that VDOT approaches the one the milestone time works out to, from 10 points below.
