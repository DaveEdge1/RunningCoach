Time goals

Current fitness: sea-level VDOT 44.6, from the 10K on Oct 10. Each goal is converted to the sea-level VDOT it needs (altitude by Daniels and the NCAA tables, and hills by Minetti et al. 2002 where the course profile is known), so goals at different altitudes compare on one scale.

About

Current fitness is the latest race or time trial, as on the Fitness tab. "Now" is what that fitness predicts at the goal's altitude (Daniels' VDOT tables, converted with both altitude methods, so it's a range), slowed by the course's hills where a profile is in data/courses. The bar fills as current VDOT approaches the VDOT the goal needs, from 5 points below it.

The mileage goal is tracked against an even pace through the year (goal × days so far ÷ days in the year). "Needed" is the weekly average the rest of the year takes; the projections add the planned miles in plan.csv, or the last 28 days' average, to what's done. Mileage counts runs only (hand-made FIT files too), never hikes or skis.
