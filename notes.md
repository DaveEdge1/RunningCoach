# Run notes

One line per run: `- YYYY-MM-DD: note` (add ` HH:MM` after the date when there were two runs that day).
A note that says "trail" marks a trail run. Grade-adjusted pace doesn't account for rough footing,
so trail runs show on the pace-at-AeT chart but stay out of its trend.

- 2026-08-20: trail
- 2026-08-25: trail
- 2026-09-05: trail
- 2026-09-13: trail
- 2026-09-19: trail, single track
- 2026-10-04: trail
